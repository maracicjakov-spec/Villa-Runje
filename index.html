Welcome to Villa Runje

A sanctuary of stone, sunlight & serenity

High in the hills above Split, about 600 metres above the sea, Villa Runje looks out over the Adriatic, its islands and the city below. It is a stone house the owner built with his own hands — private, quiet, with clean mountain air and skies full of stars.

The whole villa is yours: a heated pool and hot tub, a covered terrace made for long dinners, a garden of seasonal vegetables and a small village that still lives at its own pace — while Split is only a twenty-minute drive away.

From first light to city lights

A day at six hundred metres

Morning

  1. IMorning

    Breakfast from the garden

    Early in the morning the owner picks seasonal vegetables from the organic garden next to the house and leaves them on your table — free of charge. Espresso from the machine, breakfast under the wooden beams of the covered terrace.

  2. IIMidday

    A pool that is yours alone

    A private, heated 36 m² pool with six loungers and three hanging chairs, the Adriatic on the horizon and nobody else to share it with.

  3. IIIAfternoon

    Shade, hammock, mountain

    Sway in the hammock on the covered terrace — or take the mountain trail that starts just above the villa. Free climbing and mountain biking begin at the doorstep.

  4. IVSunset

    The golden hour

    From the pool deck and the terrace beside the bedrooms, the sun goes down over the sea and the islands. Guests come for the pool — and remember the sunsets.

  5. VNight

    Split in lights, far below

    Slip into the hot tub as the city lights come on along the coast. At this height the air is clean and the sky fills with stars.

    Location

    Discover the soul of Dalmatia

    Gornje Sitno is a small pastoral village in the mountains above Žrnovnica, on the edge of Split — friendly, quiet and unspoiled.

    Neighbours sell goat’s milk, eggs, vegetables, fruit and wine, and two restaurants serving traditional food and a minimarket are within walking distance. Above the villa, a mountain trail and climbing routes begin; below, the whole of central Dalmatia opens up.

    1. SplitDiocletian’s Palace, the old town, ferries to the islands≈ 20–30 min
    2. BeachesPebble beach at Strožanac (Podstrana), sandy beaches at Omiš≈ 15 km
    3. Omiš & the CetinaRafting, canoeing and a zip-line over the river canyon≈ 30 min
    4. TrogirUNESCO-listed medieval island town≈ 30 min
    5. Krka waterfallsNational park of travertine cascades≈ 1.5 h
    6. Island of BračA day trip by ferry from Splitday trip

    Travel times are approximate, as given by the hosts, and vary with traffic and season.
